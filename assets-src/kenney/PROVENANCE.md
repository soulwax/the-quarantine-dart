## 1. 3D Asset Kits (`assets-src/kenney/3d/`)

### 1.1 Building Kit (`building_kit/`)
Ideal for modular walls, floors, flat ceilings, doorways, and windows:
- **Walls**:
  - `wall.glb`, `wall-half.glb`, `wall-corner.glb`, `wall-corner-round.glb`, `wall-corner-diagonal.glb`
  - `wall-doorway-square.glb`, `wall-doorway-round.glb`, `wall-doorway-wide-square.glb`
  - `barricade-window-a.glb`, `barricade-window-b.glb`, `barricade-doorway-a.glb`
- **Floors**:
  - `floor.glb`, `floor-half.glb`, `floor-quarter.glb`
  - `floor-corner-diagonal.glb`, `floor-corner-round.glb`
- **Ceilings & Roofs**:
  - `roof-flat-center.glb`, `roof-flat-corner.glb`, `roof-flat-patch.glb`, `roof-flat-side.glb`
- **Doors**:
  - `door-rotate-square-a.glb` through `door-rotate-square-d.glb`
- Formats available: GLB, OBJ, FBX

### 1.2 Retro Urban Kit (`retro_urban_kit/`)
80s–90s vintage architectural and domestic exterior/interior details:
- Retro doors (`door-type-a.glb`, `door-type-b.glb`)
- Brick facades (`detail-bricks-type-a.glb`, `detail-bricks-type-b.glb`)
- Exposed cables & conduit runs (`detail-cables-type-a.glb`, `detail-cables-type-b.glb`)
- Awnings, beams, blocks, urban light fixtures, asphalt/concrete pavement patches
- Formats available: GLB, OBJ, FBX

### 1.3 Furniture Kit (`furniture_kit/`)
Domestic interior furnishings (80s–90s appropriate):
- CRT Televisions in wood cabinets (`cabinetTelevision.glb`, `cabinetTelevisionDoors.glb`)
- 80s/90s PC computer setups (`computerScreen.glb`, `computerKeyboard.glb`, `computerMouse.glb`)
- Domestic furniture: `bedSingle.glb`, `bedDouble.glb`, `bedBunk.glb`, `desk.glb`, `deskCorner.glb`, `bookcaseClosed.glb`, `bookcaseClosedDoors.glb`
- Fixtures: `ceilingFan.glb`, `bathtub.glb`, `bathroomSink.glb`, `bathroomMirror.glb`, `coatRack.glb`
- Formats available: GLTF/GLB, OBJ, FBX, DAE

### 1.4 Suburban Kit (`suburban_kit/`)
Suburban house structures and yard perimeter elements:
- House building types A through U (`building-type-*.glb`)
- Wooden and wire fences, gates, driveways, flagstone paths
- Formats available: GLB, OBJ, FBX

### 1.5 Survival Kit (`survival_kit/`)
Interactive item props matching the quarantine domestic survival setting:
- Flashlights, lanterns, batteries, radio receivers, canned food rations, first-aid kits, toolboxes
- Formats available: GLB, OBJ, FBX

### 1.6 Graveyard Kit (`graveyard_kit/`)
Atmospheric horror elements:
- Wrought iron gates, railings, stone pillars, tombstones
- Formats available: GLB, OBJ, FBX

## 2. 2D Patterns & Textures (`assets-src/kenney/2d/`)
- `pattern_pack` & `pattern_pack_extra`: Retro geometric wallpaper motifs, ceramic floor tile patterns, parquet and linoleum textures.
- `retro_textures`: Period wall and floor finishes.
- `prototype_textures`: UV calibration grids and dimensional scales.

## 3. Audio Assets (`assets-src/kenney/audio/`)
- `foley_sounds`: Footsteps on wood/stone, door creaks, latch clicks, cloth/handling noises.
- `impact_sounds`: Heavy door thuds, domestic collisions, drops.
- `retro_sounds_1` & `retro_sounds_2`: Subtle electronic cues and tactile UI tones.
